# San Lorenzo — Cantiere e villa (SL02)

The five `PANEL_*_SOURCE.png` files are the original full-resolution panels. `MASTER_ORIGINAL.png` is their 6797 × 920 stitch, before lighting changes. `MASTER_LIGHTS_REDUCED.png` keeps the same geometry while dimming selected construction lights and their pavement reflections.

`MAIN_FOREGROUND_CORRECTED.png` is the definitive high-resolution foreground. The original sky is cut across the full panorama, including the construction area; thin crane and scaffold silhouettes and the concrete buildings are retained in the foreground. `SKY_FAR.png` is reused from the preceding San Lorenzo module. `FINAL_CORRECTED.png` is a flattened visual reference, while the game uses the foreground and sky as separate layers. The runtime foreground in `public/assets/backgrounds/stage2_san_lorenzo/courtyard_v1/` is resized to 5319 × 720 for the stage.
